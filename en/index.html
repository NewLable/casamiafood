About Casa Mia — a home kitchen in Antalya

Casa Mia is a home kitchen in Antalya for those who value real taste, freshness, and quality.

We cook by hand, in small batches, and only from fresh ingredients. We do not use frozen raw materials, ready-made semi-finished products, or mass production.

Every dish is prepared to order — from kneading dough and making fillings to sauces, salads, and desserts.

The menu includes chicken, turkey and chicken-beef pelmeni; potato, mushroom and cottage-cheese vareniki; salmon ravioli; cabbage rolls, stuffed peppers, pancakes, salads, homemade pasta, cinnabons and ready meals. We make the dough and fillings ourselves: fresh meat, vegetables, eggs, cottage cheese and durum wheat flour.

We keep what matters most in home cooking: natural taste, familiar recipes, and the feeling of food made especially for you. Casa Mia is homemade food you will want to share with the people you love.

Fresh. Handmade. With care in every detail.
