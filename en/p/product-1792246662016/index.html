Beef Borscht

Hearty homemade borscht made with bone-in beef, beetroot, fresh vegetables and tomatoes. Rich in flavour, with a beautiful colour and fragrant herbs.

900 ₺

Dishes on the menu

Bone-in beef, potatoes, cabbage, beetroot, carrots, fresh tomato, tomato paste, sunflower oil, herbs, spices.

Reheat on the stove over low to medium heat, stirring occasionally, until piping hot. Alternatively, heat in a microwave-safe container.

Serve hot. Add sour cream, fresh herbs and rye bread if desired.
