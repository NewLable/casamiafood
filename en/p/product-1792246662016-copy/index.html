Chicken Borscht

Homemade borscht with chicken, beetroot, cabbage, potatoes and fresh vegetables. A comforting, aromatic soup with a traditional homemade taste.

600 ₺

Dishes on the menu

Chicken meat, potatoes, cabbage, beetroot, carrots, fresh tomato, tomato paste, sunflower oil, herbs, spices.

Reheat on the stove over low to medium heat, stirring occasionally, until piping hot. Alternatively, heat in a microwave-safe container.

Serve hot. Add sour cream, fresh herbs and rye bread if desired.
